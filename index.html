M.S. student, Neuro-Robotics Laboratory, Graduate School of Engineering, Tohoku University, Sendai, Japan

I work on contact-rich robot manipulation on real hardware: visuo-tactile imitation learning, fine-tuning and deploying vision-language-action models such as π0.5, and world-action models that predict touch. I build the whole pipeline myself, from the robot platform and data collection to training and on-robot deployment.
